Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What this site collects

Contact form: when you send a message, I receive your name, email address, and message so I can reply. The message is delivered by email through Brevo.

Visit information: the first time you visit each day, the site records the date and time, the page you opened, your IP address, your browser and device type, and the page that referred you.

Cookies and local storage: the site sets a cookie named pv_day so the visit record is only sent once per day. It also saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser. These stay on your device.

Analytics: the site uses Vercel Analytics to count page views.

How long I keep it

Contact messages are kept as long as needed to reply and follow up. Visit records are kept in my email inbox and are not shared.

Sharing

I do not sell or share your information. Brevo and Vercel process the data on my behalf to send emails and host the site.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser at any time. To ask about or delete information you sent me, email edsonedem2121@gmail.com.
